## MIT

- Short and familiar.
- Permits commercial and private use, modification, and redistribution with copyright and license notices retained.
- Includes a warranty disclaimer.
- Does not include an express patent grant.

## Apache License 2.0

- Permissive, like MIT, but longer and more procedural.
- Includes an express patent license and patent-termination terms.
- Requires preservation of notices and documentation of modified files in distributions.
- Often preferable when patent clarity matters or when organisations are expected to reuse the code.

## Decision checklist

Before adding either license, the maintainer should confirm:

1. they own or have permission to license the source code;
2. bundled product images may legally be redistributed under the chosen repository terms, or will be removed/replaced;
3. bundled fonts and third-party assets carry their required notices;
4. any collaborators agree to the licensing of their contributions.

The source-code licence decision is complete. Asset provenance remains a separate release-readiness check.
