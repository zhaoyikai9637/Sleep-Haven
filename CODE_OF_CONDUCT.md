# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SleepHaven contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologise when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour is harmful or unsafe.

To report conduct privately, use the contact method published on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/zhaoyikai9637). If no private contact method is available, open an issue requesting private maintainer contact without including names, screenshots, or sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as the small maintainer team permits. No fixed response-time guarantee is currently offered.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), but is shortened to match the project's current maintenance capacity.
